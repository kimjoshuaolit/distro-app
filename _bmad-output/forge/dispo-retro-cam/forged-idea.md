# Forged Idea — dispo-retro-cam

**What it is:** A digital disposable-camera game for wedding guests. Scan a QR → get a limited roll (25 photos + 5 short video clips, ~5–10s each) → shoot intentionally like film. Guests see their own roll; the couple receives everything, later, as a curated **montage** they control.

**Status:** HARDENED. Commissioned gift from the best man for a ~100-guest intimate wedding. Deadline: November.

## Locked decisions
- **Commissioned, not speculative.** The groom explicitly requested this (referenced a reel: disposable camera → end-of-day viewing → a *presentation* of the shots). The concept's value doesn't depend on guessing whether it's wanted.
- **Distinct value = insider POV.** Captures the wedding from where the guests stood — complements, does not replace, the hired photographer's polished outside-in coverage.
- **Delivery is later-only.** Justified: the camera runs through ceremony *and* afterparty (no same-day window), and "later" is the most authentic film-camera beat (real film = wait for prints). This consciously overrides the reel's same-day timing, on purpose.
- **The reveal is a MONTAGE.** A made thing that plays first — the groom's "presentation," relocated to private + later. Raw rolls available underneath. This is the emotional core of the gift.
- **Couple's-eyes-only.** Private by default; the couple controls whether/what gets released publicly.
- **Visibility model:** guests see their *own* roll; the couple sees *all* + controls release. *(Confirm this is intended.)*

## Rejected / owned-as-mine
- **Same-day reveal — rejected** (afterparty timeline makes it impossible; best man is occupied during the event).
- **25/5 cap — builder's design, not the groom's request.** Chosen for cost/management. Fine to keep, but own that it's a constraint reframed as a feature, not part of his ask.
- **Silent file-dump delivery — rejected.** A folder link is a hard drive, not a gift. The montage exists specifically to avoid this.

## Surviving risks (carry forward with eyes open)
1. **Montage quality is make-or-break.** "Junk rolls are charm" (true for scrolling a roll) collides with "the montage must feel special." An auto-assembled montage of ~2,500 mostly-random photos risks feeling like slop. Needs at least a light curation pass, not a pure auto-dump.
2. **Adoption.** Concept assumes ~100 guests actually scan + shoot. If few do, the insider-POV thins out. Cheap mitigation: best-man announcement + table cards.
