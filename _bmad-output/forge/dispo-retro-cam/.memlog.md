- (lock) The groom (best friend) explicitly requested this app. It is a commissioned gift, not a speculative one — neutralizes the 'unwanted gift' risk.
- (lock) Core differentiator vs hired photographer: guest/loved-ones POV — the wedding captured from the INSIDE (where guests stood) vs the pro's polished outside-in coverage. Distinct artifact, not redundant.
- (crack) TIMING CONFLICT: Groom's reference reel = disposable camera viewed END OF DAY + a PRESENTATION (shared reveal moment). Builder's plan = private per-user roll delivered a day/week later. The reveal is the emotional peak of his reference, and the current plan removes it.
- (assumption) 25/5 cap is purely the builder's design for cost/management, NOT the groom's request. The reel's inspiration was the reveal/presentation, not scarcity.
- (decision) Delivery is LATER-ONLY, justified: camera runs through ceremony AND afterparty (ends ~1am), no same-day window. Bonus: 'later' is the MOST authentic film-camera beat (real film = wait for prints/anticipation).
- (lock) Results are couple's-eyes-only; the couple controls whether/what to release publicly. Privacy default = private.
- (crack) UNPAID DEBT: groom's reel had a PRESENTATION/reveal, not just photos. Later-only delivery risks becoming a silent file-dump (link in inbox = no moment). The reveal must be STAGED, or the emotional beat he pointed at is dropped.
- (lock) The reveal = a MONTAGE (a made thing) that plays first, raw rolls underneath. This is the groom's 'presentation' relocated to private + later. Pays the reveal debt. The montage is now the emotional core of the gift.
- (crack) SURVIVING RISK 1: 'Junk rolls are charm' collides with 'montage must feel special.' An auto-assembled montage from 2500 mostly-random photos can feel like slop. The peak moment's quality is now the make-or-break — needs at least light curation, not pure auto-dump.
- (crack) SURVIVING RISK 2: Adoption. Concept assumes ~100 guests scan a QR and actually use the app. If only a fraction do, 'loved ones POV' thins out. Needs a nudge (best-man announcement, table cards).
- (note) Unresolved minor contradiction: earlier 'each guest can see their own 25 photos/1 vid' vs later 'couple's eyes only.' Reconcile: guests see THEIR OWN roll; couple sees ALL + controls public release. Confirm this is the intended model.
