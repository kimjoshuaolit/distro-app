## Intent

**Problem:** Kim can't print a QR code for the tables, and can't open or close the camera on the night except by editing times. Once a guest has joined, nothing stops them shooting, and uploads are never refused (FR4, FR18).

**Approach:**
- **Table cards and PNG.** On each event's console page, Kim can open a print page with 4 Kodak Sunset table cards per sheet (couple names, "Scan for your disposable camera", the QR) and download a high-res QR PNG. The QR encodes `<origin>/j/<eventId>`.
- **Open now / Close now.** These buttons set the window's times through a new `set-window` Edge Function, using server time.
- **Server enforcement.** The camera locks when the server says the window isn't open. Joining stays refused outside the window. Uploads of shots already taken are still accepted for **7 days after the close**, then new uploads are refused.

## Boundaries & Constraints

**Always:**
- The window is the single source of truth.
  - **Close now:** `close = now()`. If `open` is unset or not yet reached, `open = now() − 1 minute`.
  - **Open now:** `open = now()`. If `close` is unset or already past, `close = now() + 12 hours`.
- `set-window` uses the same operator gate as `save-event` (`is_operator()` with the caller's JWT), then calls a service-role-only SQL function.
- Close asks for confirmation ("Close the camera for everyone?"). Open doesn't.
- **Upload cutoff.** `reserve_shot` refuses only NEW reservations once `now() > window_close + 7 days` (`upload_closed`). An already-reserved shot (`exists`) can still finish. An unset close never refuses.
- The client treats `upload_closed` as terminal (the shot is marked rejected and stays on the device), like `cap_reached`.
- **Camera lock.** The camera locks only on a positive server answer (`ended` / `pending`). Offline or unknown never locks capture (AD-1). It re-checks on load, every 60s, on focus and when back online. A clip already recording finishes.
- **The QR.**
  - It is crisp SVG for print, with a quiet zone and error-correction level M.
  - The card page warns when the origin is localhost or 127.0.0.1 ("print this from the live site").
  - It prints cleanly on both A4 and Letter.
  - The card page is behind the operator gate.

**Ask First:** Any other upload-cutoff length. A separate override flag. Card design beyond the 4-up Kodak Sunset card.

**Never:** Refusing uploads of reserved shots, or refusing uploads while the window is merely closed (only after the 7-day grace). Gating capture on connectivity. A QR generated by a third-party web service (generate it in-browser).

## I/O & Edge-Case Matrix

| Scenario | Input / State | Expected Output / Behavior | Error Handling |
|----------|--------------|---------------------------|----------------|
| Close now during the party | open window, operator confirms | `close = now`; join refused; open cameras lock within ~60s ("The camera's closed — thanks for shooting!") | N/A |
| Late upload | shot taken during the party, phone back online 2 days later | new reservation accepted, uploads | N/A |
| Upload after the grace period | new reservation more than 7 days after the close | 409 `upload_closed`; shot marked rejected on the device | no retry loop |
| Open now | close in the past or unset | `open = now`, `close = now + 12h`; joining works | N/A |
| Guest offline at the lock check | status fetch fails | camera stays usable | N/A |
| Non-operator calls `set-window` | anon / couple / password session | 403 `not_operator`; window unchanged | N/A |
| Bad `set-window` body | missing/non-uuid id, action not `open`/`close` | 400 `bad_request` | N/A |
| Unknown event | valid uuid, no row | 404 `not_found` | N/A |
| Printing from localhost | origin localhost / 127.0.0.1 | cards render with a visible warning | N/A |

  - Shots refused with `upload_closed` were labelled "over the limit". A `rejectReason` (`cap` | `closed`) is now stored on the device shot. My Roll shows "Not saved — uploads had closed", and the chip says "N couldn't be saved".

    - The last known lock is remembered per event (an offline reload stays locked).
    - "Not open yet" shows the opening time.
    - A hidden tab doesn't poll, and there's no double check on return.

  - **Console:**

    - The lazy console chunk is wrapped in an error boundary (no blank app).

    - The card preview breakpoint is 760px (no sideways scroll).

## Design Notes

- The 7-day grace protects NFR1. Uploads only happen while the page is open (there's no background sync), so a guest with bad venue wifi may not reconnect until days later. Refusing only new reservations lets an already-started upload finish.
- The camera lock is honest UX, not security. The server enforces the join window and the upload cutoff. Within the grace period a tampered client could still shoot, but the 25/5 cap still bounds it.
- The QR is SVG so it prints at any size. The PNG (1024px) is for Kim's own designs.
